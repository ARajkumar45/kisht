# Where each recovery rule is enforced

<!-- Generated from compliance/rbi-recovery-2027.yaml by `kisht compliance --write`. Edit the YAML, not this file. -->

The Reserve Bank of India's new recovery-conduct directions (RBI/2026-27/230, issued 6 August 2026) take effect on **1 January 2027**. This page shows, rule by rule, where Kisht enforces them: the code that stops a breach and the tests that prove it. A test run fails if any row points at code or a test that does not exist.

Of the 21 rule groups traced: **7 enforced**, **9 partly** (each says what is missing), **1 not built**, 4 not applicable to a phone or chat agent.

Not legal advice. "What it requires" is a plain-English paraphrase; read the linked text before relying on it. Kisht is a demo: the lender is fictional and the borrowers are synthetic.

## Sources

- **rbi2026**: [Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026 (new Section J, paragraphs 100A-100AB)](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13672), RBI/2026-27/230, issued 6 August 2026. The directions do not mention automated, AI or pre-recorded calls. Kisht applies the rules for an "employee / recovery agent" to the AI agent. The bank version (RBI/2026-27/223) has the same paragraphs numbered 454A-454AB.
- **rbi2022**: [Outsourcing of Financial Services - Responsibilities of regulated entities employing Recovery Agents](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12378&Mode=0), RBI/2022-23/108, issued 12 August 2022. In force today; replaced for NBFCs by the 2026 directions from their effective date.
- **dl2025**: [Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=12848), RBI/2025-26/36, issued 8 May 2025. Only the paragraphs on recovery agents and grievance redressal are traced here.
